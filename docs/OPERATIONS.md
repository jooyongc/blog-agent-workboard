# Workboard operating guide

Open https://blog-agent-workboard.pages.dev/ and sign in. The active workspaces are Korea Buy List (Blogger, English and Japanese), Korea by Local, and Korea Decode. Add and edit sites in **Workspaces**. Each site owns its published URL, article path, audience, voice, topic pillars, important entities, trusted domains, saved topic templates, connection variable names, image count, quality threshold, and KST schedule.

In **Topics**, choose a saved site template, request three ranked suggestions, or add a topic. The proposer labels a Search Console opportunity only when actual connected data contains a matching query in positions 8–20; other suggestions say `strategy_only`. In **Compose**, research each language independently, review cited evidence, generate an article or edit a manual draft, inspect the SEO/AEO/GEO score and visible FAQ, and queue a reviewed article.

Automatic site schedules may select an approved topic or propose a new one. The workflow researches only the site's configured source domains, requires two confirmed sources per language, independently writes English and Japanese for Blogger, generates configured images, verifies factual claims against the research, and queues only articles meeting quality gates. Failed image/research/verification work stays for review. The cost ledger caps AI at $0.50 per article, $2 per week, and $10 per month; Cloudflare Workers AI image usage has a separate monthly asset count limit of 100.

In **Automation**, inspect upcoming times, jobs and run results; use the read-only check to see due sites. `review` work can be edited and rechecked. A partially completed remote transfer needs manual reconciliation. Blogger publication creates drafts for both languages, adds language links and matching visible FAQ structured data, verifies them, and publishes only after both drafts exist. Blogger theme `<head>` hreflang depends on theme access; body links are provided by the bridge. Native sites create a draft before switching its status to published and refuse to overwrite an existing slug.

In **Connections**, connect Blogger with Google OAuth and verify native site variables. In **Measurement**, record the raw HTML baseline and repeat after 14 days. The check reports actual HTTP status, H1, JSON-LD, canonical/noindex, robots and sitemap observations. Search Console impressions, clicks, and AI citation counts are unknown until measured with a configured source; do not interpret null as zero.

The old Aside routine `Korea Buy List 격일 신규 글 파이프라인` is paused and its next-run field was cleared during the Cloudflare handoff. The legacy CLI, `docs/ASIDE-BRIDGE-HANDOFF.md`, and ASTY material remain historical references only; the active scheduler is Cloudflare.
